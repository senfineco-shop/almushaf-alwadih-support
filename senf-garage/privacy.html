SENF GARAGE — سنفينكو جراج

Privacy policy · سياسة الخصوصية
Updated 27 September 2026

Operator and purpose

SENF GARAGE is operated by senfineco Oman for authorized staff to manage garage work. The Android package is shop.senfineco.suite. It connects to the Senfineco company workspace and requires an internet connection and an authorized company account.

Information processed

  • Company account identity, name, email, user identifier, roles and assigned branches.
  • Customer names and phone numbers, vehicle registration plates, vehicle details and service history entered by staff.
  • Work orders, inspection notes and photos, invoices, payment records and other documents entered or uploaded for garage operations.
  • Changes to records and the identity and time of the staff member making a change. Hosting and sign-in providers also process technical request information needed to operate and secure the service, such as IP addresses and browser or device information.

Use, storage and access

Information is used for account access, customer and vehicle service, inspections, invoicing, shared workshop operations and security. Business records and inspection photos are transmitted to company cloud services. Cloudflare provides hosting, storage and access services; company sign-in may use Google Workspace. Configured business integrations, including Daftra, may receive relevant customer, invoice and payment data when those workflows are used.

Access depends on the employee's company, branch and role permissions. Authorized staff can search the shared customer directory and service history; branch financial access is restricted separately. Information is not a public social feed. Exporting, printing or sharing a document at your request produces a copy that you control.

Device storage and security

The app uses local storage and session cookies to maintain the workspace and sign-in state. Network connections use HTTPS and the service checks company access permissions. Signing out removes the app session and local web storage; it does not delete business records retained on the company server or copies previously exported.

Advertising and permissions

The Android app does not include an advertising SDK or request the Android advertising identifier. It does not request location access. Photos and documents selected or supplied for garage work are processed as part of the corresponding record.

Retention and deletion requests

Business records, audit information and historical versions can remain in company storage after a user signs out or an employee account is disabled. There is no automatic deletion of server records when the app is uninstalled. For access, correction, account removal or deletion requests, contact info@senfineco.center with the subject “SENF GARAGE privacy request”. Identify the company account or relevant record; do not send your password. Requests require identity and authorization verification. Records needed for accounting, legal obligations or resolving disputes may need to be retained; the request response should identify applicable limitations.

Audience and changes

The app is intended for adult staff, not children. Changes to its data practices will be reflected on this page. Contact: info@senfineco.center.
